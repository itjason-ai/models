01 — When it comes up

Why companies call me about ISO 27001

ISO 27001 is the internationally recognized certification for an information security management system.

A customer or partner requires certification

Buyers outside the US, and many large US enterprises, ask for an ISO 27001 certificate the way others ask for a SOC 2 report.

You hold a certificate to the 2013 version

The transition period to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 ended on October 31, 2025. A certificate still issued against the 2013 version needs to be recertified to the current standard.

You want one program that answers many questionnaires

A certified ISMS gives you a defensible answer to a large share of customer security questions, and its controls map well to SOC 2 and other frameworks.

02 — How the work runs

How I build it

Five steps, built with your team so the system keeps running after the certificate is issued.

01

Scope and gap assessment

Define what the ISMS covers and measure where you stand against the standard and Annex A. You get a gap report and a realistic timeline to certification.

02

Risk assessment and treatment

A documented risk methodology, a risk register, and a treatment plan. This decides which controls you need and why, and it’s the first thing an auditor tests.

03

Build the ISMS

Policies, procedures, and controls written for how your company actually operates, plus a Statement of Applicability that ties each of the 93 Annex A controls to a decision.

04

Internal audit and management review

Both are required before certification, and both are commonly left too late. They’re completed and documented before the certification body arrives.

05

Stage 1 and Stage 2 audit support

I prepare you for both stages, support you during the audits, and work through any nonconformities until the certificate is issued.

04 — Questions

Common questions

Straight answers to what clients usually ask first. Anything else, bring it to the call.

Do you issue the certificate?

No. Certification is issued by an accredited certification body after its own Stage 1 and Stage 2 audits. My role is getting you ready to pass, and keeping the consulting and certifying roles separate is what an accredited body requires.

What happens after certification?

A certificate runs on a three-year cycle, with surveillance audits in the intervening years and a recertification audit at the end. The ISMS has to keep operating between audits, which is where a retainer usually fits.

What changed in the 2022 version?

Annex A was reorganized into 93 controls in four themes (organizational, people, physical, and technological), with new controls such as threat intelligence, cloud services, and secure coding.

How long does ISO 27001 take?

It depends on scope and starting point. The Readiness Assessment gives you a realistic timeline, including when to book the certification body.
