02 — Frameworks

Every major framework, one way of working

Different requirements, the same discipline: what you’re actually obligated to do, a ranked gap assessment, controls written for how you operate, and evidence that holds up.

SOC 2

For B2B SaaS and service companies whose enterprise customers want an independent report on their controls.

ISO 27001

For companies selling internationally or to buyers who require an accredited certificate for the ISMS.

HIPAA

For healthcare providers, health plans, and the software and service vendors that handle their patient data.

CMMC / NIST 800-171

For defense contractors and subcontractors that handle federal contract information or controlled unclassified information.

PCI DSS

For merchants and service providers that store, process, or transmit payment card data, or can affect its security.

NIST CSF

For companies that need a defensible security program and a common language for boards, insurers, and customers.

GLBA / FTC Safeguards

For mortgage lenders and brokers, fintechs, auto dealers that arrange financing, tax preparers, and other non-bank financial institutions.

Not sure where you stand? Start with a Readiness Assessment ($6,500 fixed) against one framework; 100% of the fee is credited toward an implementation signed within 30 days. Certification and attestation are issued by independent auditors and certification bodies; my role is getting you ready to pass.

04 — Process

How engagements work

No open-ended hourly arrangements. Scope and price are agreed before work starts.

01

Discovery call

Thirty minutes. What's driving the deadline, what's already in place, and what you're actually obligated to do — which is often narrower than you've been told.

02

Written scope of work

A fixed fee against defined deliverables, each with a stated completion standard. You know what you're getting and what it costs before anyone signs.

03

Built, documented, handed over

The program is yours to run when I'm done — editable policies, a live risk register, and a compliance calendar. Retainer support afterward is optional, not assumed.

Every engagement is scoped and priced to what the situation actually requires. Retainers are scoped separately.

How I handle your data: I use business AI tools that don’t train on client data, and every deliverable gets a human review from me before it reaches you.
