02 — Requirements

What applies, in plain terms

Most firms face more than one of these. The control work overlaps, so the program is built once.

GLBA and the FTC Safeguards Rule

A written information security program for non-bank financial institutions, with a Qualified Individual, a written risk assessment, specific required safeguards, and an annual report to the board. How I build it.

NYDFS Part 500

New York’s cybersecurity regulation for DFS-licensed entities: a CISO, a risk assessment, multi-factor authentication, asset inventory, incident notification, and an annual certification of compliance.

FFIEC expectations

The examination standards banks and credit unions are held to, which also reach their technology service providers through vendor management.

IT general controls

Access, change management, and operations controls your financial statement auditors test. Weak ITGCs turn into audit findings.

SOC 2

The report your institutional clients and bank partners ask for when they review you as a vendor. How I build it.
