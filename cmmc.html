01 — When it comes up

Why companies call me about CMMC and NIST SP 800-171

CMMC verifies security requirements that most defense contracts have carried for years. It shows up in three common ways.

A prime contractor asked for your SPRS score

Primes are flowing down DFARS 252.204-7012 and CMMC requirements, and they want to see your NIST SP 800-171 assessment score before awarding or renewing a subcontract.

A solicitation includes a CMMC level

Level 1 covers federal contract information under the 15 basic safeguarding requirements in FAR 52.204-21. Level 2 covers controlled unclassified information under the 110 requirements of NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2. Level 3 adds requirements from NIST SP 800-172 for the most sensitive programs.

Your score was posted years ago and nobody has looked since

A self-assessment score has to reflect your environment as it is today, and CMMC self-assessments come with an annual affirmation of continued compliance.

04 — Questions

Common questions

Straight answers to what clients usually ask first. Anything else, bring it to the call.

Is CMMC cancelled?

No. Phase 2 was suspended in July 2026 and the program is under review, but the CMMC rule remains in effect, Phase 1 self-assessment requirements still appear in contracts, and DFARS 252.204-7012 still requires NIST SP 800-171.

Can we have open items and still qualify?

Only in a limited way. Under the CMMC rule, conditional Level 2 status requires a minimum score of 88 out of 110, only certain requirements can remain on a plan of action, and open items must be closed within 180 days.

Are you a C3PAO?

No. Certification assessments are performed by authorized third-party assessment organizations or by the government. I prepare you for them, and keeping those roles separate avoids conflicts of interest.

What is the difference between NIST SP 800-171 and NIST CSF?

NIST SP 800-171 is a specific set of 110 requirements for protecting CUI. The NIST Cybersecurity Framework is a broader, outcome-based framework for running a security program. Many contractors use both.
