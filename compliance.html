02 — Approach

The same five steps, every framework

Requirements differ. The way a defensible program gets built doesn’t.

01

Obligations first

What you are actually required to do, by whom, and by when. It is often narrower than you have been told, and it sets the scope for everything else.

02

Gap assessment

Where you stand against the requirements, with gaps ranked by risk and by what an auditor or customer will test first.

03

Build what’s missing

Risk assessment, policies, and controls written for how your company operates, and nothing you will never use.

04

Evidence

Records that show the controls run, organized the way assessors and customers ask for them.

05

Audit and review readiness

Preparation for the auditor, assessor, examiner, or customer review, and support while it happens.
